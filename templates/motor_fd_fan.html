Overload (51) Time-Current Characteristic

GE Multilin "Standard" thermal curve: T = CM x 2.2116623 / (0.025303373x(M-1)² + 0.050547581x(M-1)), M = current as a multiple of motor FLA.

Starting/Stall Margin Check

The overload curve should pass BELOW both Safe Stall markers and ABOVE both Start markers — the relay must not trip during a normal start, but must trip before insulation is thermally damaged on a stall.
